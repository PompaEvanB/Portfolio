On this page I will go over what sledge is, its story, my level design work on the project and my decision making process for levels one.

About Sledge

Sledge is a first person action platformer about a human destroying robots that have invaded heaven. Sledge is directly inspired by Neon White, Ultrakill, and Team Fortress 2. It's for speedrunners and people who enjoy flowstate in platformers.

Pillar of Sledge

Sledge had one core pillar: Fast Satisfying Platforming. The objective of every element was to give the player a choice on how to quickly beat the level. Skipping large portions of a level is a reward. The game was designed for speed running.

What Sledge is to me

Sledge was my capstone project in my final year of college. I worked on it with my friend Anthony Molle who created the concept and served as lead director on the project for a while. This was my first big project working with over ten people as a developer.

Level 1

Core Level Goals

Level 1 is meant to serve as an introduction to the core mechanics of the game: walking, jumping, and using your hammer for movement and fighting enemies. Outside of introductions, the goal of this level is to invite the player to move quickly and to force them to think about their time.

Using your hammer

By using the hammer players can launch themselves in any desired direction. They can maintain their momentum by launching themselves repeatedly while in mid-air. The faster they go, the better their score. The hammer can also hit enemies, bounce off enemies, and parry projectiles.
